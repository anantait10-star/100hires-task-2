# B2B SaaS Cold Outreach Research

## Project Goal

This repository collects and organizes public content from B2B SaaS cold outreach practitioners.

The goal is to build a high-signal research corpus that can later be distilled into a practical outbound playbook covering:
- account targeting
- prospect research
- messaging
- social selling
- cold calling
- pipeline generation
- GTM execution

## Research Methodology

The research process is source-first and operator-focused:

1. Identify credible practitioners who actively teach or execute outbound systems.
2. Collect public content from LinkedIn, YouTube, podcasts, and other relevant sources.
3. Store raw or lightly formatted material in the appropriate research folders.
4. Track source links, context, and selection rationale in `research/sources.md`.
5. Use the collected material to extract recurring principles, frameworks, tactics, and objections.

The focus is on practitioners with real operating experience, not generic marketing commentary.
