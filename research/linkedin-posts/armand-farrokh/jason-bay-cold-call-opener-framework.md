# Research Notes

## Main idea

Effective cold call openers reduce resistance by acknowledging the interruption and creating permission before pitching.

Both frameworks avoid starting with a hard sell and focus on making the prospect comfortable enough to continue the conversation.

## Pipeline Stage

Cold Call Execution → Opening → Permission → Conversation Start

## Key Framework

1. Permission-Based Opener
- Acknowledge that the call is unexpected
- Be transparent that it is a cold call
- Ask for a small amount of time before explaining the reason for outreach

2. "Heard The Name Tossed Around" Opener
- Creates familiarity before pitching
- Tests existing awareness of the company
- Starts a conversation instead of immediately explaining features

## Potential Playbook Use

Use as cold call opener examples when designing outbound sequences.

These frameworks can help SDRs increase early-call engagement by lowering buyer defensiveness during the first few seconds of a conversation.
