# Original Post

### How To Get Higher Response Rates With LinkedIn Video DMs

I get **25–30% response rates** on LinkedIn video DMs.

I book **1 meeting for every 10 videos** I send.

Our clients in The Quota League see similar conversions across different geos & verticals.

You’ll get the same if you follow these steps:

---

## LinkedIn Video DM Workflow

1. Select 5 target accounts for the week.

2. Go deep into researching those accounts.

3. Select 5 buyer personas per account (25 total).

4. Scan all 25 profile pages for personalization.

5. Save your research notes somewhere mobile-accessible.

6. Write a 100-word elevator pitch video script.

7. Block 2 hours for video prospecting twice a week.

8. Go outside for a walk. Record all videos in bulk.

9. Record & save the videos with your iPhone.

10. Upload them via LinkedIn mobile app to DMs.

---

## Why Video DMs Get Insane Reply Rates

### 1. Pattern Interrupt

99% of sales reps don’t send videos.

So if you do, it’s an easy way to stand out.

Walking outside signals buyers that you’re busy and out there doing things.

Also less boring than sitting in a home office.

---

### 2. Reciprocity

Buyers are impressed by the effort you put in when you record a personalized video.

They feel like they owe you a response.

Even if the response is negative.

---

### 3. Trust Accelerator

87% of human communication is non-verbal.

Buyers ignore cold emailers.

They try to get out of cold calls from strangers.

All because AI and automation have broken trust.

---

But videos give them so many data points:

* Your voice & tonality
* Your body language
* Your facial expressions
* Your environment & attire

That’s why video accelerates trust faster than any other channel.

# Research Notes

## Main idea

High-quality prospecting requires focused account research and intentional execution rather than high-volume generic outreach.

Deep understanding of fewer target accounts can create more relevant conversations.

## Pipeline Stage

Account Selection → Research → Personalization → Outreach Execution

## Key Framework

1. 5x5 Account Research System

- Select a limited number of high-value accounts
- Research multiple buyer personas inside each account
- Build context before outreach

2. Batch Prospecting Workflow

- Separate research time from execution time
- Prepare messaging before outreach blocks
- Create repeatable prospecting habits

3. Trust-Based Outreach

- Use personalized communication
- Increase familiarity through human signals
- Differentiate from automated outreach

## Potential Playbook Use

Use for account-based outbound motions.

Instead of maximizing activity volume, prioritize accounts, understand context, and execute focused outreach.
