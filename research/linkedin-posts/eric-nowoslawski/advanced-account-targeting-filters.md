# Research Notes

## Main idea

Modern outbound targeting can go beyond basic company size, industry, and job titles.

Using website data, AI attributes, business models, and search signals allows teams to create more precise account lists before outreach begins.

## Pipeline Stage

Market Research → Account Selection → Prospect List Building

## Key Framework

1. Website Intelligence
- Analyze website content, keywords, pages, and positioning
- Identify companies based on what they actually do

2. Business Model Segmentation
- Segment accounts by business type
- Identify SaaS, ecommerce, marketplace, agency, or other models

3. AI-Based Attributes
- Detect relevant company characteristics
- Use attributes like API availability, pricing model, demo availability, and content activity

4. Search-Based Discovery
- Find companies based on market presence
- Discover accounts through search behavior and ranking signals

## Potential Playbook Use

Use to create high-quality account lists before launching outbound campaigns.

Better targeting increases outreach relevance before personalization or copywriting begins.
