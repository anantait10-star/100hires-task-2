# Original Post

1 positive response for every 90 contacts on completely cold contacts is a welcome sight these days.

Here's what's going on with this campaign.

---

First, this campaign is working so well not only because the customer has a good offer.

They are targeting an industry that rarely gets any kind of good cold emails.

None of these emails were found using any LinkedIn data-based contact database.

We built the whole database ourselves by:

* Scraping Google Maps
* Mining websites for emails

So already, their inboxes are far less busy.

---

This campaign in particular also has **3 levels of personalization:**

## 1. Google Review Analysis

Analyzing their Google reviews for complaints about a particular service.

If any complaints mention our service keywords, they are qualified for the campaign.

Then we make the offer:

> We can guarantee the complaint with that service won't happen again.

---

## 2. Role-Based Messaging

The messaging changes based on:

* The role we found on the website for the email
* Whether it's a generic email

---

## 3. Referral-Based P.S. Line

Our P.S. line asks for a referral to another contact we found on the site in case they aren't the right person.

---

Packaged together, this means:

We are only going after people where we can prove:

* They have experienced the pain before
* Customers have complained about it publicly online

Then we:

* Segment based on role so each person gets the best message for them
* Name drop someone else if they aren't responsible for that area

---

**Offer is by far the most important.**

When we tested just a regular campaign with only spintax, it still got:

**1 lead for every 250 emails.**

So the AI variables doubled the lead rate.

But again:

**Offer is always king.**


## Pipeline Stage

Data Collection → Signal Detection → Segmentation → Personalized Outreach

## Key Framework

1. Unique Data Sources
- Build custom prospect databases
- Avoid oversaturated contact sources
- Find less competitive channels

2. Pain Signal Detection
- Analyze external signals such as reviews or public data
- Identify prospects with evidence of existing problems

3. Segmented Personalization
- Adjust messaging by role and context
- Use AI variables based on meaningful signals

4. Offer Quality
- Strong targeting improves results
- Personalization amplifies good offers but does not replace them

## Potential Playbook Use

Use as a framework for AI-assisted outbound campaigns.

Start with finding accounts that show evidence of pain, then personalize outreach around that signal.
