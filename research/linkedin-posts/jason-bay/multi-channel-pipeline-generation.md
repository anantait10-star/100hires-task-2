# Original Post

## Post 4 — Cold Outbound Is Only One Type of Pipeline Generation

"Cold outbound" is only **one flavor of pipegen.**

It's the coldest, least effective way to engage buyers.

I'm not saying it doesn't work.

We've built out our entire business at Outbound Squad by helping companies like Shopify, Rippling, Zoom, etc. crush at it.

But it's the very last thing you should do.

---

Mitch Thomas runs enablement at Brooksource for 180+ large enterprise AEs with **zero BDR support.**

They self-source all of their pipeline.

Do they make cold calls?

Yes.

But it's not the only way their very best reps source pipeline:

1. Referrals
2. Community events & philanthropy
3. Industry associations
4. Tradeshows & conferences
5. Following champions to new businesses
6. Asking for introductions
7. In-person drop-bys

---

Phone, email, and LinkedIn are just tools.

You can choose to have ice-cold conversations, or you can **warm them up by looking for a better path in.**

When we worked with Brooksource, they were already converting **22% of their outbound calls into qualified meetings.**

After we worked together, it was **35%+.**

I've never seen it higher with any other client.

---

I'd love to take more credit for it, but a big part was calling into leads that weren't completely cold.

If there's one way to virtually guarantee that you land more meetings, it's to do just that.

# Research Notes

## Main idea

Pipeline generation is broader than cold outreach.

Cold calls, email, and LinkedIn are only channels. Strong outbound teams increase conversion by creating warmer paths into accounts before reaching out.

## Pipeline Stage

Account Research → Relationship Building → Outreach → Meeting Creation

## Key Framework

1. Cold Outbound
- Direct phone, email, and LinkedIn outreach
- Works best when combined with context

2. Warm Pipeline Sources
- Referrals
- Industry communities
- Events
- Champion tracking
- Introductions

3. Better Paths Into Accounts
- Find existing connections or signals
- Reduce friction before starting conversations

## Potential Playbook Use

Use when designing outbound pipeline systems.

Before defaulting to cold outreach, identify warmer entry points that increase the likelihood of starting a buyer conversation.
