# Original Post

If I were looking to hire my next all-star sales rep, here's exactly what I'd focus on...

We've worked with 250+ sales teams and over 10,000 reps in the last 5–6 years.

Teams as large as:

* Shopify
* Zoom
* Rippling

And teams with 3–5 reps rolling out an outbound motion for the first time.

20,000+ reps.

The best reps fit into one very specific archetype:

1. The Grinder
2. The Procrastinator
3. The Cyborg
4. The Owner

---

## Archetype #1 — The Grinder

A grinder never misses their activity target.

They muscle their way through pipegen.

They're in the top 25%, but never the top 10%.

---

## Archetype #2 — The Procrastinator

The procrastinator only builds pipeline at the beginning of every quarter...

When they don't have any.

They're last.

---

## Archetype #3 — The Cyborg

The cyborg leverages every AI tool imaginable.

They're a modern-day version of the grinder.

But they never develop a **big book of business** because:

* Their buyers don't respond to AI-written emails
* They don't like to pick up the phone

---

## Archetype #4 — The Owner

The best archetype we've seen is:

**The Owner.**

This rep acts as the CEO of their territory.

They:

### 1. Disqualify

Ruthlessly disqualify opportunities unworthy of their:

* Energy
* Time
* Attention

---

### 2. Offer

They are consultative and make strategic “gives” to buyers early and often to earn their time.

---

### 3. Provoke

They engage buyers with a strong point of view on their problem that compels action now.

---

And they book **4.3x more meetings** than their average-performing peers.

They know that in a world full of AI:

**They must be too good to ignore.**

The Owner is the archetype you should be hiring for in your next best rep.

Agree or disagree?

# Research Notes

## Main idea

Top-performing sales reps do not only increase activity volume. They operate their territory like business owners by prioritizing the highest-value opportunities and creating stronger reasons for buyers to engage.

## Pipeline Stage

Territory Strategy → Pipeline Generation → Opportunity Prioritization

## Key Framework

1. Grinder
- High activity volume
- Relies mainly on effort and consistency

2. Procrastinator
- Creates pipeline only when needed
- Lacks consistent pipeline discipline

3. Cyborg
- Uses automation heavily
- Risks losing human relevance and buyer connection

4. Owner
- Thinks strategically about territory and pipeline
- Disqualifies low-quality opportunities
- Creates valuable offers
- Brings strong points of view to buyers

## Potential Playbook Use

Use as a framework for designing outbound behavior.

High-performing outbound systems require more than volume or automation. Reps should combine strategic account selection, valuable outreach, and disciplined execution.
