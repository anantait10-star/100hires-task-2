## Why Our SDRs Sit Inside Marketing

Our SDRs sit inside Marketing.

And our pipeline has never been stronger.

---

ClickUp's product-led motion creates a massive self-service user base.

That generates tens of thousands of signals every day:

* Adoption & usage patterns
* Expansion triggers
* Hand-raises

The list is 100+ signals long.

---

SDRs manage all of the outreach against those signals.

And it's a constantly moving target.

The signals change.

The plays have to change with them.

---

Having our:

* SDRs
* Demand gen
* Ops
* Marketing leadership

In one org means feedback loops close in **hours, not weeks.**

That velocity is how we get every ounce of leverage out of every:

* Signal
* Lead
* SDR

---

It's not about who “owns” SDRs.

It's about where they can move the fastest.

And therefore:

**What moves the business the fastest.**

For us, it’s marketing.

---

P.S.

The primary growth path for our SDRs is into sales.

And the majority of the training the individual SDRs get is around sales acumen.

So we have to maintain a tight partnership with sales leadership to make sure we're developing reps who are ready for their next step while they’re generating pipeline.

# Research Notes

## Main idea

Pipeline generation improves when teams are structured around fast feedback loops.

The placement of SDR teams matters less than whether sales, marketing, and operations can quickly act on buyer signals.

## Pipeline Stage

Signal Generation → SDR Execution → GTM Feedback Loop → Pipeline Creation

## Key Framework

1. Signal-Based GTM Motion

- Use product usage, intent, and engagement data
- Turn buyer signals into targeted SDR actions

2. Cross-Functional Alignment

- Connect SDR, demand generation, operations, and marketing teams
- Reduce delays between insight and execution

3. Fast Feedback Loops

- Continuously update outbound plays based on performance
- Adapt messaging and targeting as signals change

## Potential Playbook Use

Use when designing revenue organizations.

High-performing pipeline systems require alignment between teams generating demand, managing signals, and executing outreach.
