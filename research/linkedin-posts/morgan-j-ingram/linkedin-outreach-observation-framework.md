# Original Post
### The AMP Formula For Better LinkedIn Outreach

I analyzed **127 failed LinkedIn outreach attempts** across SDR teams I've worked with.

The pattern that kept showing up cost these companies hundreds of qualified meetings.

---

First off…

I have been there.

Early in my sales career, my messages were painful.

Yes, I used:

> "Hope all is well... My name is Morgan. We have synergies and I'd love to connect."

Just absolutely terrible.

But I realized something:

They weren't ignoring **me.**

They were ignoring my **approach.**

That changed everything.

---

So when I looked at the 127 messages from other reps...

I noticed they were doing the same thing as me.

Here's the framework I developed after studying hundreds of messages:

## The AMP Observation Outbound Formula

1. **Observation**
   → Show them you know them

2. **Context**
   → Why this observation matters

3. **Pain Point**
   → What they're likely facing

4. **Value Prop / Power Move**
   → How you help

5. **Call To Action**
   → Next step

---

## Example

### Before:

> "Hope all is well..."

(Almost barfed writing this.)

---

### After:

> "Hey...
>
> Saw your product launch at the conference last week and the AE/SDR openings.
>
> Not sure if you have created sales messaging for the new product in your outbound yet or if it's still a work in progress.
>
> A CRO I know in your space doubled BDR headcount post-launch and still missed plan because the motion was not set.
>
> Curious how you're thinking about pipeline generation with the new product?"

---

See the difference?

The first one says nothing.

The second one proves a human connected the dots between:

* Their product launch
* Their hiring
* A real problem they might be facing

---

Every successful message I've seen follows this pattern.

As Samantha McKenna says:

> "Show Them You KNOW Them before you show them what you DO."

When I follow this framework, response rates jump.

When SDR/AE teams I coach use this inside Sales Team Six, they start more conversations on LinkedIn which leads to more pipeline.

---

Your prospects are waiting for someone who gets them.

The question is whether your team knows how to connect the dots to show up that way.

# Research Notes

## Main idea

Successful LinkedIn outreach is not based on generic introductions or immediate pitching.

Effective messages show that the seller understands the prospect's context before introducing their solution.

## Pipeline Stage

Prospect Research → Social Outreach → Conversation Creation

## Key Framework

AMP Observation Outbound Formula:

1. Observation
- Identify something specific about the prospect or company
- Show research before asking for attention

2. Context
- Explain why the observation matters
- Connect information into a relevant business situation

3. Pain Point
- Connect the situation to a possible challenge
- Create relevance before pitching

4. Value Prop / Power Move
- Explain how you can help based on the context

5. Call To Action
- Create a simple next step

## Potential Playbook Use

Use when creating LinkedIn outbound messages.

Move from generic personalization to connecting real prospect signals with relevant problems and conversations.
