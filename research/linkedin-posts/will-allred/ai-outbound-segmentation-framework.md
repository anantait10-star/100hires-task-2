# Research Notes

## Main idea

AI should not be applied equally across every outbound account.

The level of automation should depend on account value, buying signals, and expected deal size.

## Pipeline Stage

Account Segmentation → AI Assistance → Outbound Execution

## Key Framework

1. Tier 1 Accounts
- Highest value accounts
- Use AI for research assistance
- Keep human-written and reviewed messaging
- Prioritize personal calls and touches

2. Lower Tier Accounts
- Higher automation is acceptable
- Use AI to increase execution speed
- Less manual personalization required

3. Continuous Learning
- Analyze content and activity data
- Improve future outreach decisions

## Potential Playbook Use

Use when designing AI-assisted outbound systems.

AI should increase seller effectiveness without replacing strategic thinking for high-value prospects.
