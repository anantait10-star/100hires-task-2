# Original Post
Sellers, this pyramid is your market.

Most prospecting messaging is optimized for the green.

If you'd stop talking about yourself, you'd 3x your opportunity zone.

"But Will, I get a 5% reply rate with a simple pitch of our software."

Great...

Sounds like you have:

Good product marketing
A well-targeted list for that message

This advice will still help.

The biggest mental flip in this pyramid is that it's from the buyer's perspective.

Their mindset:

I'm buying now
I'm open to it
I'm not thinking about you
I'm not interested

You're just an interruption in their mindset.

You can get a lot out of very targeted messaging that boils down your pitch to a hyper-simple:

"We do X. Solves Y. Interested?"

But...

Your ceiling is capped.

Demand creation > demand capture is how you get out of that cap.

The framework:

"If ___ is true, would it help if you could ____?"

Pain is the name of the game.

Validate that it exists.

Then you can carry the conversation forward.

# Research Notes

## Main idea

Most outbound messaging only targets buyers who are already actively looking.

Better prospecting expands the opportunity pool by creating demand: helping prospects recognize a relevant problem before pitching a solution.

## Pipeline Stage

Buyer Understanding → Problem Framing → Message Creation

## Key Framework

1. Demand Capture
- Targets buyers already looking for a solution
- Works with clear product pitches
- Limited by existing buyer awareness

2. Demand Creation
- Starts with buyer problems instead of seller features
- Helps prospects identify pain
- Creates conversations before active buying intent exists

3. Pain Validation
- Test whether a problem exists
- Use problem-based questions before introducing the product

## Potential Playbook Use

Use when writing cold outbound messages.

Instead of only explaining what the product does, structure messaging around problems buyers recognize and want to solve.
