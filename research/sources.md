# Sources

This file tracks B2B SaaS outbound practitioners selected for future content collection. The focus is on people and teams with practical outbound experience: cold email, cold calling, SDR leadership, prospecting systems, signal-based selling, sequencing, and pipeline generation.

## Selection Methodology

These experts were selected because together they represent the complete outbound pipeline:

- Data & Account Intelligence
- Prospect Research
- Personalization
- Cold Email Copywriting
- Multi-channel Sequencing
- Social Selling
- Cold Calling
- Follow-up Systems
- GTM Strategy

The goal is to study practitioners who actively build, run, teach, or optimize outbound systems, not generic marketing influencers. Each source was chosen for its connection to practical pipeline creation: finding the right accounts, researching prospects, writing relevant messages, sequencing across channels, starting live conversations, following up, and connecting outbound activity to broader SaaS go-to-market strategy.
