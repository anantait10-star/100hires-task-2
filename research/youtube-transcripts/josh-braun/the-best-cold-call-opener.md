# The Best Cold Call Opener

I just saw a post on the 37 best coold
call openers or maybe it was 42 I can't
remember you know what's more important
than what you say your tone your tone
determines how confident prospects think
you are and selling is a transfer of
confidence yet most salespeople when
they make coold calls sound like this hi
I know you weren't expecting my call but
I was hoping you can help me out for a
moment they have this like uptone at the
end which makes you sound needy and
desperate and Confused instead take it
down on octave talk a little lower like
this hey James my name is Josh we've
never spoken before I was hoping you
could help me out for a moment hey Linda
this is Josh I know you weren't
expecting my call do you have a moment
downward inflection it's the difference
between this you're considering getting
a divorce and this you're considering
getting a divorce turn your questions
into statements
